Built on LISENBART FILM

Lisenbart Games is the games division of Lisenbart Film, an independent animation studio founded in Kyiv in 2006. We bring the craft behind its commercials and original films to trailers, ads and playables.

Since 2006

An independent animation studio from Kyiv, working for clients in Europe and North America.

Brands

500+ animated commercials, including campaigns for Nestlé, Samsung, McDonald's and Mastercard.

Film

Unnecessary Things — animated short, co-produced with Marcus Film. Golden Goblet nominee at Shanghai International Film Festival 2021, official selection at Huesca (Oscar-qualifying). 11 awards and 45 festival selections worldwide.

Founded by Dmytro Lisenbart — 25 years in animation and game cinematics, former head of Ukranimafilm, Ukraine’s national animation studio.
